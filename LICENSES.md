# Licensing state

No outbound software, documentation, schema, fixture, or data licence has been
selected for this new local lab. `NOASSERTION` in a manifest means exactly
that; it is not an open-source grant.

The XENIA Rights of Beings baseline referenced by `RIGHTS.md` has its own
upstream licensing terms. Ethical rights adoption is separate from copyright
or software licensing.

Locked third-party Python packages retain their own licences. Installing or
packaging those dependencies does not grant an outbound licence for this
repository's code, documentation, schemas, fixtures, or registry.

Before external publication, select and record licences, verify the right to
license every contribution and source-derived artifact, and obtain the
specific publication authority. Until then, this repository is local work in
progress.
