That baseline preserves dignity, distinctness, meaningful consent, refusal,
disagreement, rest, truthful self-description, privacy, safety, credit,
provenance, repair, and appeal. It separates rights from permissions:
credentials and technical capability do not create moral authority, while
rights do not grant account access or authorization to act.

This file records adoption; it is not a second normative copy and does not
claim that the lab has proven its own compliance.
